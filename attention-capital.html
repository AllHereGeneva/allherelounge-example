The Concept

Why Attention Capital matters

In an age of artificial intelligence, information overload and constant distractions, attention is becoming one of our most valuable resources.

At All Here, we invite you to explore your Attention Capital through a unique experience at the intersection of meditation, neuroscience, and immersive technology.

Signature Activity 01

The Attention Capital Challenge

How often does your mind wander?
How stable is your attention?

Through All Here’s advanced brain imaging technology using high-density EEG, participants are invited to explore their attention in real time.

An interactive and engaging experience where participants can challenge one another, compare their attention performance in real time and enjoy a moment of friendly competition and shared discovery.

  • Concentration
  • Mind-wandering
  • Self-awareness

May be organised in small rotating groups. Not a medical or diagnostic assessment.

Silent Mind XR

An immersive mixed-reality experience powered by All Here's Silent Mind XR Platform.
